The Cookie & Portal Nexus

Combine cookies with multi-tab mastery

1
Add the Access Cookie
Use context.addCookies() to add cookie nexus-pass = "open" to the current browser context. This grants access to the nexus portal.
2
Open the Nexus Portal
Click the button below to open a new tab. The new tab reads the same browser context cookies and will show different content depending on whether nexus-pass is present.
Portal ready — cookies are shared across tabs
3
Verify the Portal Content
In the popup page, verify that #portal-access-msg contains "Nexus Access Granted". This only appears when the cookie was properly set before opening the portal.
